About Us

Welcome to Mensa App!

We’re a dedicated group of students who share a passion for good food and a desire to enhance the dining experience in Tübingen.

Our platform allows you to explore the menus of local mensas and cafeterias, complete with analytics and honest reviews. Our goal is to connect students with the best dining options available. Thank you for joining us on this journey!
